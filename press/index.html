Press release

Bubble Punks™ is out now: a three-minute party battle game for iPhone, iPad and Android

Trap your rivals in bubbles, pop them, and be the last crew standing, online or on one Wi-Fi with no internet needed.

November 24, 2026. Immersia Labs today launched Bubble Punks™, a party battle game for iPhone, iPad and Android, in the US. Players drop gum bubbles, catch rivals in the splash, and pop them before their crew comes to the rescue. Rounds last three minutes at most, so there's always time for one more.

Play with your crew. Find a match on a team or solo, team up with up to 3 friends, or open a room for up to 8 players with a shareable code. Phones on the same Wi-Fi can play together with no internet needed, made for Thanksgiving week, when friends and families are in the same room. Practice against computer players any time.

Punks with attitude. Riot, Volt, Moss and Sunny settle who's boss in Drip City, a neon night city, across three arenas: Neon Rooftops, Toy Factory and Ice Arcade. Conveyor belts flip direction, ice sends you sliding, and thirteen items drop mid-fight.

Popped isn't out. In online matches, a popped Punk can climb onto the Ledge, the roof's edge around the board, and keep playing from there until the round is over.

Looks, never power. Season 0 is on: players earn new frames, poses, titles and colors as they play. What you earn or unlock changes how you look, never how you play. Bubble Punks costs nothing to download, with in-app purchases, and has no ads.

Bubble Punks is out now on the App Store and Google Play in the US, in English: App Store · Google Play

About Immersia Labs. Immersia Labs is an independent studio making its own games and apps: Bubble Punks, a pocket-sized party battle game, and Infinite Reading, where the story sets the scene. For iPhone, iPad and Android.

How each Punk is got

  • Riot: playable from the start.
  • Volt: a welcome gift after your first online match.
  • Moss: earn him free at the last tier of Season 0's free rewards, get him at once with the paid Season 0 pass, or unlock him for 300 Glowdrops, the game's currency, which comes in packs.
  • Sunny: play him in Practice from day one. Own him by reaching the top of the paid Season 0 pass.
  • Practice offers all four Punks. Online and Wi-Fi matches use the Punks you own.
